Bundlekit Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 20, 2026

Bundlekit ("the App") provides product bundling services to merchants who use Shopify to power their stores. This Privacy Policy describes how personal information is collected, used, and shared when you install or use the App.

1. Personal Information the App Collects

When you install the App, we are automatically able to access certain types of information from your Shopify account: product data (titles, prices, images) and store configuration data.

We do not collect personal information directly from merchants or their customers. We do not access customer names, emails, phone numbers, or addresses.

2. How Do We Use Your Personal Information?

We use the information we collect to provide the Service and to operate the App (creating product bundles and applying discounts).

3. Sharing Your Personal Information

We do not share your personal information with third parties.

4. Data Retention

We retain the data we collect for as long as your store is using the App. If you uninstall the App, we will delete the data associated with your store.

5. Contact Us

For more information about our privacy practices, or if you have questions, please contact us by e-mail at kingsleyneddy@gmail.com.
